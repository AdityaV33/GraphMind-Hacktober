# FDA Phase II Investigational New Drug Protocol: Compound NX-109

## Protocol Overview
Protocol IND-8842 defines the human clinical trial for compound NX-109, a selective kinase inhibitor intended for neurodegenerative treatment.

## Dosing Regimen & Tolerability
- The target maintenance dosage for adult cohorts is established at 50 mg administered daily.
- Maximum tolerated dose prior to Phase II expansion is capped at 75 mg daily.

## Principal Investigators & Facilities
- Principal Investigator **Dr. Miriam Chen** leads the clinical operations at the **Charité University Hospital**.
- Charité University Hospital is located in **Berlin, Germany**.
- The primary compound supply is manufactured and distributed by **Bavaria BioTech** in Munich.
