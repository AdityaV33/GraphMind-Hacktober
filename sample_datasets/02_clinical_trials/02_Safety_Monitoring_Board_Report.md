# Independent Data & Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) Interim Report

## Safety Alerts & Protocol Revisions
Following elevated liver enzyme markers observed in Cohort B, the DSMB has ordered an emergency modification to Protocol IND-8842.

## Mandated Dosage Reductions
- The target maintenance dosage for compound NX-109 is strictly reduced to 25 mg administered daily.
- Dosages exceeding 35 mg daily are halted across all participating trial sites.

## Clinical Oversight & Site Transfer
- Principal Investigator **Dr. Miriam Chen** has transferred clinical trial oversight to **Dr. Hans Meyer**.
- Bavaria BioTech in Munich has ceased shipment of 50 mg formulations and transitioned production to 25 mg capsules.
