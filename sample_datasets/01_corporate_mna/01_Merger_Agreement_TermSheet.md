# Mergers & Acquisitions Term Sheet: Apex Holdings & NovaBio

## Executive Summary
Apex Holdings has finalized the non-binding acquisition framework for NovaBio Therapeutics for a total transaction value of $420,000,000 in cash and equity considerations.

## Key Personnel & Leadership
- **Dr. Aris Thorne** is designated as the Vice President of Genomic Discovery following the closing of the merger.
- Dr. Aris Thorne directly directs the **Omics Research Laboratory**, which is based in the Cambridge BioHub.
- The **Cambridge BioHub** is situated in Cambridge, Massachusetts, and holds proprietary patents for CRISPR editing vectors.
- Chief Financial Officer **Elena Rostova** approved the transaction on behalf of Apex Holdings.

## Facilities & Assets
- NovaBio maintains its secondary research facility, the **Helios Biosafety Level-3 Unit**, within the Zurich Innovation Park.
- The Helios Biosafety Level-3 Unit is managed by Senior Virologist **Klaus Weber**.
- Approved initial R&D expenditure for NovaBio's oncology division is fixed at $45,000,000.
